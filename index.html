Grilled Salmon with Lentil Tabbouleh

~52g protein·~14g fiber

Ingredients
    Steps
    1. 1Rinse lentils. Add to pot with water (2× lentil volume), bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer 18–20 min until tender but not mushy. Drain, spread on a tray to cool.
    2. 2Combine cooled lentils, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and parsley in a bowl. Dress with lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, and salt. Toss and let sit while you grill.
    3. 3Pat salmon dry. Brush with olive oil, season with remaining salt and pepper.
    4. 4Heat grill or grill pan to medium-high. Grill salmon skin-side down 4 min, flip, cook 3–4 min more until flesh flakes easily. Do not move it until it releases naturally.
    5. 5Spoon tabbouleh into bowls, lay salmon on top. Squeeze extra lemon over everything.
    Tip: Lentils can be cooked a day ahead. Green or French (Puy) lentils hold shape best. Salmon done at 125–130°F internal for medium.

    Ingredients
      Steps
      1. 1Preheat oven to 400°F. Lightly oil a baking dish large enough to hold all pepper halves upright.
      2. 2Rinse quinoa. Add to pot with equal volume water, bring to boil, cover, reduce to low. Cook 12–15 min until water is absorbed. Fluff with fork.
      3. 3Place pepper halves cut-side up in baking dish. Roast 10 min to soften slightly. Remove, keep oven on.
      4. 4Heat olive oil in skillet over medium-high. Add garlic, cook 30 sec. Add ground turkey, break apart, cook until no pink remains, ~5 min.
      5. 5Add diced tomatoes, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper to skillet. Stir, simmer 2 min. Fold in spinach until wilted, then stir in cooked quinoa.
      6. 6Pack filling into par-baked pepper halves, mounding generously. Top each with cheese. Bake 15 min until cheese is melted and bubbly.
      7. 7Let sit 5 min before serving. Pairs well with hot sauce or plain Greek yogurt.
      Tip: Filling can be made a day ahead — stuff and bake from the fridge, add 5 min to bake time.

      Shrimp + Edamame Stir-Fry with Brown Rice

      Ingredients
        Steps
        1. 1Whisk together soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and red pepper flakes. Set aside.
        2. 2Cook brown rice per package instructions. Set aside.
        3. 3Heat 1 tsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add brown rice, press flat, cook 3–4 min until lightly crisped. Transfer to a bowl.
        4. 4Wipe skillet, add 1 tbsp oil over high heat. Add shrimp in a single layer, cook 90 sec per side until pink and curled. Remove to a plate.
        5. 5Add remaining oil. Add red bell pepper, stir-fry 2–3 min. Add snap peas and edamame, cook 2 min more. Add garlic and ginger, toss 30 sec until fragrant.
        6. 6Return shrimp. Pour sauce over everything, toss to coat, cook 1 min until glazed.
        7. 7Divide brown rice between bowls, top with stir-fry. Garnish with green onions. Serve immediately.
        Tip: High heat is key — don't crowd the pan or shrimp steam instead of sear. Brown rice adds ~40g carbs vs cauliflower rice.

        Ingredients
          Steps
          1. 1Whisk together lemon juice, olive oil, Dijon, garlic, salt, and pepper in a large bowl until emulsified.
          2. 2Add cannellini beans to bowl. Toss to coat in vinaigrette, let sit 5 min — beans absorb flavor best when dressed first.

          Spiced Chickpea + Chicken Thigh Skillet

          ~42g protein·~19g fiber
          Servings3
          Rating
          🍳 Cooked it! 0
          99Cal/100g
          7.9gProtein
          5.3gCarbs
          5.0gFat
          1.3gFiber
          Ingredients
            Steps
            1. 1Mix cumin, smoked paprika, turmeric, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Pat chicken dry, rub all over with half the spice mix.
            2. 2Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken smooth-side down 4–5 min until deep golden. Flip, cook 3 min more. Remove to a plate.
            3. 3Reduce heat to medium. Add garlic, cook 30 sec. Add chickpeas and remaining spice mix, stir to coat, cook 2 min until chickpeas start to crisp.
            4. 4Add zucchini, toss with chickpeas, cook 2 min. Pour in broth, scraping up any browned bits.
            5. 5Nestle chicken back in. Cover, reduce to medium-low, cook 18–20 min until chicken reaches 165°F.
            6. 6Uncover, push chicken aside, fold in kale in batches. Stir until wilted, ~2 min.
            7. 7Squeeze lemon over skillet. Taste, adjust salt. Serve directly from pan.
            Tip: Don't move the chicken once it hits the pan — let it release naturally for a proper sear. Leftovers reheat well with a splash of broth.
